Changelog

The main changes in each release. Keep your Mac and iPhone on the same version; the Mac app checks for updates automatically.

1.5.0 LATEST

November 23, 2026
  • iPhone: fixed the app getting stuck searching after returning from the background, which previously needed a force quit.
  • iPhone: the tutorial is gone and the app opens straight to Home; until a Mac is paired, Home shows the website address to share or copy to your Mac.
  • Mac: a custom vocabulary makes names, product names and technical terms more accurate, and polish keeps their spelling; it stays on your Mac.
  • Mac: text history keeps the raw and polished text of every round, searchable and copyable, so nothing that failed to type is lost.
  • Mac: the app, installer and network name are now VoxChord; existing pairings and permissions keep working.

November 11, 2026
  • iPhone: during a long recording the home screen gently closes in from the edges with soft blur and colour fringing, then springs back when you send or settles when you cancel.
  • iPhone: with Reduce Motion on, a soft edge vignette replaces the deformation.

1.4.8

November 10, 2026
  • iMic is now VoxChord: the new name and pixel wordmark appear across the iPhone home screen, onboarding and permission prompts, and the Mac menu bar, windows, settings and alerts.

1.4.7

November 4, 2026
  • iPhone: stays with the Mac you used last instead of jumping to another paired computer, and paired Macs can now be removed from the device list.
  • iPhone: more reliable reconnection after returning from the background; recordings are no longer cancelled when the system adjusts the microphone format.
  • Mac: fixes for saving settings, protecting credentials, pairing and recognition stability.

1.4.6

November 3, 2026
  • iPhone: Apple voice processing (noise reduction, echo cancellation, auto gain) is on by default, a stalled microphone recovers by itself, and a new MIC TEST checks your input.
  • iPhone: onboarding now teaches hold, release and pull-to-send on the real home screen, offline and without the microphone, and can be replayed any time.
  • Mac: Model Studio endpoints are easier to enter, and newer key formats are accepted.
